07 / Before you ask

Six things people ask before they book.

Do I need to already be on HighLevel?

No. If you are on it, I build inside your sub-account. If you are not, part of the first call is working out whether it is the right tool for what you are doing. Sometimes it is not, and I will say so.

Can my team run it after you leave?

That is what the handoff is for. Every workflow carries notes explaining what each step does, and you get a one-page document covering the whole system. If your team needs a developer to change a follow-up email, I built it wrong.

What if I only need one thing fixed?

Then that is what you pay for. A broken workflow does not require a retainer to look at.

How do you charge?

A one-time fee for the build, invoiced on completion. Any retainer afterwards is optional and priced separately.

Where are you, and does that matter?

Davao, Philippines. I work US hours and overlap most European schedules. Everything in the work section was delivered remotely.

Do you have client testimonials?

Not for HighLevel work yet. This is a newer part of what I do, and I would rather show you the builds than borrow someone else's words. Everything in the work section is mine, and I can walk you through any of it live on the call.
